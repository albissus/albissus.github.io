The name

albissus

/ælˈbɪsəs/ · an invented word — a bright, mysterious light, often associated with knowledge.

Always lowercase as a logo. In running prose it takes a normal capital: “Albissus builds…”. Never all-caps, never camel-cased, never hyphenated. It is a singular entity — “Albissus is”, not “Albissus are”.

One-line descriptor, used verbatim in bios, decks and directory listings:

Albissus builds immersive VR simulations, digital media, and marketing for organisations that dream big.
Logo

A wordmark and a pulse

The identity is two parts: the lowercase albissus wordmark set in IBM Plex Mono 600, and the three-dot mark — a stand-in for a signal, a heartbeat, a loading state.

Wordmark — primary
albissus
Three-dot mark — pulses on a 2.4s stagger

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of one full character of the wordmark. Minimum wordmark size is 90 px wide on screen (≈24 px cap height); below that, use the three-dot mark alone.

albissus

Colour

The wordmark appears in exactly three colours: Signal yellow on dark grounds, Ink or Paper on the opposite ground. Nothing else.

Do

  • Set it only in IBM Plex Mono 600, lowercase.
  • Signal yellow on Ink / black; Ink on Paper.
  • Give it a full character-height of clear space.
  • Use the dot mark alone when space is tight.

Don't

  • Put the yellow wordmark on Paper — 2.1:1 contrast.
  • Add glow, bevel, gradient, outline or drop shadow.
  • Re-space, stretch or recolour the three dots.
  • Substitute another mono or a sans face.

Proportion

Roughly 70% ground (Ink or Paper), 25% neutrals and type, 5% Signal yellow. If a screen looks yellow, it has too much. The accent marks the one thing that matters on a given view — the primary action, the live state, the keyword.

Typography

Mono for structure, sans for sentences

IBM Plex Mono sets everything structural — the wordmark, headings, labels, buttons. IBM Plex Sans carries running text. The mono gives the brand its engineered, terminal-adjacent character; the sans keeps paragraphs comfortable.

Rules

  • Sentence case for headings and buttons. UPPERCASE only for eyebrows and metadata labels, always letter-spaced.
  • Keep running text near 65–70 characters per line.
  • Load only the weights in use: Mono 400/500/600, Sans 400/500.
  • Fallbacks: ui-monospace, Menlo, monospace and -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif.
Voice & tone

Plain-spoken. Confident. Quietly playful.

Albissus is a small team that does ambitious technical work. The writing should sound like a person who knows the craft explaining it without inflating it — short sentences, concrete nouns, the occasional dry aside.

Do

  • “We build immersive VR simulations, digital media, and marketing.”
  • “Six ways we bring your ideas to screen — or headset.”
  • “A small team, built for this specifically.”
  • “Tell us what you’re trying to build.”

Don't

  • “We leverage cutting-edge XR to unlock synergies.”
  • “Our Comprehensive Service Portfolio.”
  • “A world-class team of industry veterans.”
  • “Contact us today for a free consultation!”

Conventions

  • Second person for the reader (“your idea”), first-person plural for Albissus (“we build”).
  • Serial comma. Em dashes for asides, spaced en dash if you must.
  • One exclamation mark per project, maximum. Usually zero.
  • “VR”, “3D”, “XR” are fine unspelled; avoid coining new acronyms.

Boilerplate — long

Albissus is a digital media studio building immersive VR simulations, 3D web applications, neurofeedback tools, and the video and marketing that carry them. We work with organisations in education, training, and research that want to put something ambitious in front of people — on screen, or in a headset.
Motion

One ambient pulse, everything else on interaction

Motion in the identity is restrained. The three-dot mark breathes; a few elements respond to the pointer; the hero types itself once. Nothing scrolls on its own.

Pulse — opacity 0.35→1, scale 0.82→1, 2.4s ease-in-out, dots staggered 0.25s.

  • Hero eyebrow types on once (per-character), the caret blinks, then it rests. It does not loop.
  • Phonetic spelling flips letter-by-letter — rotateX, 0.09s per tick.
  • Buttons lift 1 px on hover; cards lift 3 px with a soft Ink shadow.
  • Transitions are 0.15–0.2s ease. No bounce, no spring.
  • No parallax, no scroll-jacking, no autoplaying video or audio.
  • Everything collapses to ≤0.01ms under prefers-reduced-motion.
Layout & components

A 1080 px column and hairline edges

Content sits in a centred 1080 px container with a 1.75 rem gutter. Sections alternate Ink and Paper grounds and are separated by a single hairline, never a heavy rule.

Applications

Three-dot mark, Signal yellow on Ink, centred, no wordmark. Supply 16, 32, 180 and 512 px. Dots stay static in the icon — no motion.
